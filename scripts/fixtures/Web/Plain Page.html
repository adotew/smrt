Key formula

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √dₖ) V

Press Space in a deck to flip a card; the scaling term is √dₖ.

Attention is all you need.
Why divide by √dₖ?

Without it, large dot products push softmax into regions with vanishing gradients.
